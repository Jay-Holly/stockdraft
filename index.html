How To Play
Fantasy football for the stock market. Read this once. Then go win.
📋
🎮 Ways To Play
FREE
Solo AI Practice League
You vs 3 AI managers — The Analyst, The Gambler, The Crypto King, The Contrarian. No fee, no credit card. Learn the game before you bring in real friends.
PRIVATE
League With Friends
Start a private league, get an invite link and 6-digit code, send it to your crew. 4 to 12 players. Commissioner pays the platform fee. Your first league is full price — every league after that is 50% off.
PUBLIC
Play Against Strangers
Join the queue, pick your league size and fee tier, get matched with other players automatically. Like video game matchmaking — but for stock picking.
📖 The Rules
1
The Draft
$1,000,000 fantasy salary cap. 10 rounds × $80K on stocks. Shares = $80K ÷ current price. Then a $200K crypto flex pool across up to 3 slots (BTC, ETH, SOL, DOGE) — you choose how to split it.
2
Weekly Matchups
Head-to-head every week. Your score = combined % gain of your starters Monday 9:30 AM to Friday 4:00 PM ET. Higher % wins. Your record determines playoff seeding.
3
Bench & IR Swaps
2 bench spots. Bench doesn't score but still tracks. Once per week before Monday lock, swap a bleeding starter to the bench and call up a bench stock.
4
Waivers & Trades
Drop and add undrafted stocks. Trade with other managers before the deadline. After the deadline — no more moves.
5
Weekly Awards
Every Friday: Rookie of the Week (+$2K), Lottery Hit (+$2K), The Contrarian (+$3K), Diamond Hands (+$5K), Sweep Week (+$2K). And the Bench Curse (-$1K) — announced publicly. Bonuses hit your crypto pool.
6
Playoffs
Top 4 by record. Semifinals week 1, championship week 2. Tiebreaker: season total %. The champion takes the pot — whatever your league agreed to on the side is between you.
7
League Chat
Every league has its own message board. Trash talk. Trade negotiations. Award reactions. Find it in My Team → League Chat.
⚖️ StockDraft is a fantasy game for entertainment and education. All portfolios are fantasy — no real trading, no investment advice. Platform fees are for app access only. Side bets between players are handled outside the app. 18+ only.

📖 The Rules
1
The Draft
You get a $1,000,000 fantasy salary cap. Spend $80,000 per pick across 10 rounds on stocks — shares = $80K ÷ current stock price. Then allocate your $200,000 crypto flex pool across up to 3 crypto slots (BTC, ETH, SOL, DOGE). You choose how to split it.
2
Weekly Matchups
Every week you go head-to-head against another manager. Your score = the combined % gain of your starting stocks and crypto from Monday 9:30 AM to Friday 4:00 PM ET. Higher % wins. Market holidays = shorter week, same rules.
3
Bench & IR Swaps
You have 2 bench spots. Bench stocks don't score but they still track — so you'll see exactly what you missed. Once per week before Monday lock, you can IR swap: bench a bleeding starter and call up a bench stock. Use it wisely.
4
Waiver Wire & Trades
Drop a stock, add an undrafted one. Propose trades with other managers before the trade deadline. Trades can be vetoed by the commissioner within 24 hours. After the deadline — no more moves.
5
Weekly Awards & Bonuses
Every Friday after close, awards fire automatically. Rookie of the Week (+$2K crypto bonus), Lottery Hit (+$2K), The Contrarian (+$3K), Diamond Hands (+$5K), Sweep Week (+$2K) — and the dreaded Bench Curse (-$1K, announced publicly). Bonuses go straight into your crypto flex pool.
6
Playoffs & Champion
Top 4 managers by record make the playoffs. Semifinals week 1, championship week 2. Tiebreaker: season total % gain. The champion takes the pot. Whatever your league agreed to on the side — that's between you.
7
League Chat
Talk trash, negotiate trades, react to awards. Every league has its own message board — find it in My Team → League Chat. This is where the game actually happens.
